## Assessment judgement

The direction is defensible under the recorded brief: an implemented interaction with explicitly simulated AI behaviour, a system diagram, two or three illustrated examples, rationale and development evidence. The brief accepts simple sketches or screenshots; Excalidraw is a suitable tool choice, not a recorded requirement. The HTML pack is a review aid, not the required single report. Meeting categories does not establish a mark.

The simulation is the central scope argument. A2 develops the interaction needed for A1's proposed comparison; it does not conduct that comparison. Scripted advice and authored flags must not be presented as live inference, retrieval or error detection. The curated passage also removes much of the effort of finding a relevant lecture section discussed in A1. A claim-specific warning can hint at the answer. Equal word counts do not equate information, difficulty or rendering height. Reusing one question limits coverage and prevents treating replays as independent trials. Task 02 is a no-targeted-flag baseline with identical reminders, not an active specificity comparison.
